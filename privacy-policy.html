Privacy Policy

Arrow Puzzle — Android puzzle game

Effective date: 3 October 2026 · Version 1.0

The short version. Arrow Puzzle is a single-player puzzle game. It has no account, no server, and no analytics. Your progress and settings stay in app storage on your device. Advertising is served by Google AdMob, which does collect data — in regions where the law requires it, you are asked for consent before any ad is requested.

1. Who we are

Arrow Puzzle is published as an open-source Android app. It is not operated by a company or a server: there is no backend, no user account system, and no first-party analytics or crash-reporting SDK in the app.

2. Information stored on your device

Arrow Puzzle saves the following settings locally, using Android's private app storage (DataStore Preferences). This data never leaves your device and is never sent to us:

  • Highest level you have unlocked
  • Your best star rating for each level
  • Whether sound and haptic feedback are enabled
  • Whether you have seen the first-play tutorial
  • Your chosen launch animation
  • Your chosen language (or “follow device”)

Progress is not backed up to a cloud account and is not synced between devices. Uninstalling the app deletes this data permanently. Clearing the app's storage in Android Settings does the same.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of Arrow Puzzle is supported by ads served by Google AdMob, Google's advertising platform. Arrow Puzzle shows three ad formats:

  • Banner ads — displayed on the home and level-select screens.
  • Interstitial ads — shown after at most every second completed level, before the next-level action. Never on launch, never on exit, and never while you are playing.
  • Rewarded video ads — entirely optional. You choose to watch one to regain a life after failing a level. Nothing is granted for clicking an ad, only for a video that plays to completion.

What AdMob collects

Google, as the ad provider, may collect the advertising ID, IP address, device and OS model, app version, coarse location derived from IP, ad interactions, and diagnostic information such as ad-request and crash logs. Google uses this to serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud. Google acts as an independent controller for this processing; its handling is governed by the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Partner Data Policy, not by this policy.

AdMob's own details are here: How Google uses data and AdMob privacy-related policies.

4. Consent and how to change it

Before any ad is requested, the app asks Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) whether consent is required in your region and, where it is, shows a consent form.

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, you are shown a consent form and ads are only requested after you agree.
  • In other regions, no prompt is shown.

You can revisit and change your answer at any time from Settings → Privacy Options inside the app. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it also resets your consent choice. Google's consent mechanisms, including the IAB TCF where applicable, are described in the Google Partner Data Policy.

If you decline, the game remains fully playable; ads are simply not requested.

5. Google Play Games

Arrow Puzzle optionally asks Google Play Games whether you are signed in, so it can display your Play Games name on the home screen. This is read once at launch and is never required:

  • If you have no Play Games account, no Play Store, no network, or you decline, the game simply runs as a guest and you lose nothing.
  • The app does not upload your level progress, star ratings, or any gameplay data to Play Games, and Play Games cannot change them. There is no cloud save and no achievements or leaderboards are posted.
  • The only screen that can ever appear is Google's own sign-in screen, and only when Google decides one is needed.

If you sign in, Google's handling of your Play Games data follows the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Permissions the app requests

  • INTERNET — used only to download ads from Google and to check whether Play Games is reachable. The game itself plays fully offline.
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — used to avoid ad requests when the device is offline.
  • VIBRATE — used for the optional haptic feedback when you tap a tile. This is a setting you can turn off in-game.

The Google Mobile Ads library also declares the advertising ID permission (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID) as part of its own manifest.

7. Children

Arrow Puzzle is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed at children under 13. It contains no user-generated content, no chat, no purchases, and no location-based features. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us at the address below and we will help.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or export your personal data, to object to processing, and to lodge a complaint with a regulator (for example the GDPR rights in the EU/UK, or the CCPA/CPRA rights in California).

Arrow Puzzle holds no server-side copy of anything about you. Your in-game progress is on your device, and you can erase it at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it. Data held by Google as the ad provider or the Play Games provider is subject to Google's own privacy practices; you can reset your advertising ID, opt out of personalised ads, and manage ad-personalisation settings through Google My Account, Google Ad Settings, or the aboutads.info opt-out page.

9. Security

All game data lives in Android's private app storage, which is isolated per app by the operating system. Communication with Google for ads and sign-in uses HTTPS. There is no account to compromise because there is no account.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL with a new effective date, and the change will be noted in the app's release notes on Google Play. Continuing to use the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.

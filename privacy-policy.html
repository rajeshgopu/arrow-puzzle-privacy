Privacy Policy

Arrow Puzzle — Android puzzle game

Effective date: 11 October 2026 · Version 1.1

The short version. Arrow Puzzle stores game progress and settings locally on your device. Depending on the services enabled in the version you use, Firebase may process app usage, diagnostics, performance information, and push-notification tokens. Google AdMob is used to serve and measure advertisements and may process advertising and device-related data. Google Play Games may be used for sign-in or to display your player name if that feature is enabled.

1. Who we are

Arrow Puzzle is an Android puzzle game. This policy explains how information may be handled by the game and by third-party services integrated into it, including Google Firebase, Google AdMob, and optionally Google Play Games. The game developer does not operate a separate game backend unless a future version explicitly adds one.

2. Information stored on your device

The game may save gameplay progress and preferences in Android app storage, such as:

  • Highest level unlocked and best star rating for each level
  • Sound and haptic-feedback preferences
  • Whether the first-play tutorial has been shown
  • Selected launch animation and language preference

Locally stored progress is not necessarily synced to a cloud account. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage may delete locally stored progress. If a future version adds cloud saving, this policy will be updated.

3. Firebase services

The app may use the following Firebase services when they are included and enabled in the installed version:

  • Firebase Analytics: may collect app interactions, usage events, device information, app instance identifiers, and related diagnostic or measurement data to help understand how the game is used.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: may collect crash reports, stack traces, app state, device details, and technical diagnostic information to identify and fix crashes.
  • Firebase Performance Monitoring: may collect app startup, network request, and performance trace information to help identify slow or unstable behaviour.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM): may process an app-instance or registration token and message-delivery information to send push notifications if notifications are implemented and enabled. The app may not send notifications in every version.

Firebase data handling is governed by Google's applicable terms and policies. Learn more in the Firebase Privacy and Security information and the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of Arrow Puzzle uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Depending on the version and ad configuration, the game may display banner, interstitial, and optional rewarded ads.

Google and its advertising partners may process information such as the advertising ID, IP address, device and operating-system information, app interactions, ad views or clicks, approximate location inferred from IP address, and diagnostic or fraud-prevention signals. The exact data used may depend on your device settings, region, consent choices, and Google's advertising configuration.

Google's processing is described in the Google Privacy Policy, How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services, and AdMob privacy information.

5. Advertising consent and privacy choices

Where required, the app uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) or another applicable consent flow to present advertising privacy choices. The choices shown and the ads that can be requested depend on your region, applicable law, and the app's current configuration. Do not assume that declining personalised advertising means all ads are disabled; non- personalised or limited ads may still be available where permitted.

If the app provides a Settings → Privacy Options entry, you can use it to revisit available consent choices. You can also manage advertising ID and ad-personalisation settings through Google Ad Settings and your Android device settings.

6. Google Play Games

If Google Play Games features are enabled, the app may check sign-in status or display your Play Games profile name. Any data handled by Google Play Games is subject to the Google Privacy Policy and the settings and permissions associated with your Google account. Only describe this feature as enabled if it is present in the released version.

7. Permissions

Depending on the build, the app may request or use permissions and capabilities such as:

  • INTERNET — network access for ads and enabled Google services.
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — to check network connectivity where needed.
  • VIBRATE — for optional haptic feedback.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS — if the app sends notifications on Android versions that require this permission.
  • com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID — may be declared by the advertising SDK for advertising-related functions.

The exact permissions depend on the final app manifest and the SDK versions included in the release.

8. Children

Arrow Puzzle is a general-audience puzzle game. The developer does not knowingly use the game to collect personal information directly from children. Advertising and analytics settings must be configured appropriately for the app's actual target audience and the applicable laws. If you believe information has been handled inappropriately, contact the developer using the details below.

9. Data sharing, retention, and deletion

The developer does not sell personal information. Information may be processed by Google through Firebase, AdMob, or Google Play Games as described above. Retention and deletion of data processed by those services are subject to the relevant Google service settings, policies, and retention practices. Local game data can generally be removed by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app. You can manage Google account and advertising choices using Google My Account.

10. Security

Android app storage is protected by the operating system's app-isolation mechanisms. Communications with Google services generally use encrypted network connections. No internet-connected service can guarantee absolute security.
